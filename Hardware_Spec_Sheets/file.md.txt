All odom boards share the following common components: 
Mcu: esp32-c6-dev-kit from waveshare 
Mux: TCA9548A 1-to-8 I2C Mux 
Opticals: VL53L0X Time-of-Flight (ToF)


The following components are exclusive to V1: 
Gyro: WitMotion WT901 9-axis gyro


The following components are exclusive to V2: 
Gyro: EVAL-ADXRS453Z
